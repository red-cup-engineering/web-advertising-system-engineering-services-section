Only then change that title's source record to `live`. Keep the original [package release](https://github.com/red-cup-engineering/web-advertising-system-engineering-services-section/issues/3) and [host receiving](https://github.com/red-cup-engineering/house-ads-server-site/issues/1) obligations. The native tests exercise all seven original records in their inactive state.
